# Addendum to the post labelling instructions, for the House (14 November 2026)

Read `classify/rubric_prompt.md` first; this adds to it and wins where they differ.
Read "senator" as "representative".

- **Misuse only is C, not B** (Oz, 12 November 2026). If the only catastrophic harm a post
  names is misuse of AI by people (terrorists, criminals or hostile states using AI to make
  biological, chemical, nuclear or cyber weapons), label it C and add the flag
  `misuse_only`. B needs a catastrophic danger from AI systems themselves (for example
  loss of control), or catastrophic risk stated without limiting it to misuse.
- **A general mention of "public safety" or "national security" risk is D**, unless a
  catastrophic or serious danger from AI itself is described.
- Label each post on its own text, as before. Do not adjust for the date of the post or for
  the person's other posts; that is done later.
